Wheel Zoom & Drag

All demos

Scroll the wheel over the chart to zoom about the pointer, and drag to pan. Shift-drag draws a box to zoom to; the keys +, -, the arrows and 0 zoom, pan and reset. The zoom and pan state is Inkproof.Web's Nav over Inkproof.Viewport's View; the lines are uploaded to the GPU once and every view draws the same buffers.
