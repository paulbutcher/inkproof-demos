Gradients

All demos

Horizontal

A line whose colour changes along the time axis, switching abruptly from one colour to the next.

Over and under

A line that is blue where the value is below zero and red above it, switching exactly at zero.

Banded

A line coloured in three horizontal bands by value, each band a single colour.

Fills

Two filled areas whose colour follows the value: one turns red where it is high, the other where it is low.

Relative fill

A fill whose colours are tied to the lowest, middle and highest values of the data, so they follow whatever range the data has.
