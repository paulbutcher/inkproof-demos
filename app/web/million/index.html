A million points

All demos

One million samples of noise with twelve spikes, each one sample wide, drawn into 1120 pixel columns. The top chart keeps one sample per column, as naive decimation does. The bottom chart draws the lowest and highest sample of each column, which Inkproof's pyramid returns exactly: Inkproof.Core.Pyramid.getElem_columns says each column is the query of its range, and Inkproof.Core.Maximum.fold_get_eq_some says the maximum of a range is one of its samples and at least every sample in it, so no spike can be lost. Red rings mark where the spikes are.
