All demos

The chart shows a coarse copy of 5,000 samples, one in twenty, fetched from the site. Drag to zoom to a range (x only): the page then fetches just the samples of that range, as a byte range of the data file, and redraws them. The reset button, or a drag back out, fetches the coarse copy again. uPlot's demo swaps in a fixed four points; the data here is a fixed hash.
