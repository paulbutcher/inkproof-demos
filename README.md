# Inkproof.Demos

Part of Inkproof. Released under the Apache 2.0 license; see `LICENSE`.

The [uPlot demos](https://leeoniya.github.io/uPlot/demos/index.html), recreated with the Inkproof libraries and published at https://paulbutcher.github.io/inkproof-demos/. An application, not a library: nothing depends on it. Each release tag is a version of the site.

## What it does

- Show the Area Fill demo, three series with filled areas and linear axes, from uPlot's page of that name: `Inkproof.Demos.AreaFill.chart`, `Inkproof.Demos.axes`, at `area-fill/`, with a legend giving each series at the cursor under the plot, a dotted cursor with a marker on each series and a click on an entry hiding its series; `?seed=<n>` fixes the random data (`Browser.seed`).
- Show the Points demo, four charts of random walks with markers and gaps, from uPlot's page of that name: `Inkproof.Demos.Points.multiParts`, `Points.singleParts`, `Points.sparseParts`, at `points/`, a legend click drawing again from retained layers.
- Show the Scatter & Bubble demo, 200 bubbles sized by area on two y axes and four clouds of 10000 markers, from uPlot's `scatter.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.Scatter.bubbleChart` and `scatterChart`, at `scatter/`, with a static legend naming the clouds, a click hiding one without tessellating or uploading the others again (`Scatter.cloudTraces`, `Scatter.cloudDisplay`); `?seed=<n>` fixes the random clouds.
- Show the Sparklines demo, ten stocks' volumes and closing prices as 150 by 30 charts sharing one WebGL context, and the Sparklines (w/ floating bars) demo, a line with floating bars either side of zero, from uPlot's `sparklines.html` and `sparklines-bars.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.Sparklines.chart`, `SparklinesBars.chartSplit`, at `sparklines/` and `sparklines-bars/`.
- Show the Multi-Bars and Grouped and stacked bars demos, bars over categories with values written at their ends, per-bar colours, separate scales per series and stacked or horizontal layouts, from uPlot's `multi-bars.html` and `bars-grouped-stacked.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.Bars.Chart`, `MultiBars.benchmark`, `MultiBars.series`, `GroupedBars.charts`, at `multi-bars/` and `grouped-bars/`, with legend entries named as on uPlot's pages (`Bars.Series.name`, `Bars.Line.name`, `Scatter.Cloud.name`) and axes drawn as uPlot's faint ones (`Axes.style`), axis labels set in one text style (`Axes.text`) and bar labels, legend and tooltip formatted by one number format (`Axes.numbers`); `?seed=<n>` fixes the random durations and benchmark line of Multi-Bars.
- Show the Latency Heatmap demo, 35k latencies of a skew normal as translucent cells, counted in 5 ms buckets coloured by hue, and as histograms, from uPlot's `latency-heatmap.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.LatencyHeatmap.samplesDisplay`, `bucketsDisplay` and `Inkproof.Demos.LatencyHistogram.chart`, at `latency-heatmap/`, with a legend reading each column's, or each bucket's, counts at the cursor (`Browser.valueGroup`, `Browser.groupsAccess`).
- Show the Heatmap / Scatter demo, 45k latencies counted in 15 second by 2 ms cells, from the same page: `Inkproof.Demos.HeatmapCells.display`, at `heatmap-cells/`.
- Show the Timezones & DST demo, six days of hourly data in UTC and in London and Chicago across each zone's 2019 daylight-saving changes, from uPlot's `timezones-dst.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.CalendarTime.dstSpecs`, `Zone.offset`, `Spec.chart`, with the day or month context line under the ticks from `Spec.tickContexts`, at `timezones-dst/`.
- Show the Months demo, three years of monthly levels with ticks on the first of each month and the year line under them (`Spec.tickContexts`), from uPlot's `months.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.CalendarTime.monthSpec`, at `months/`; `?seed=<n>` fixes the random data.
- Show the ArcSinh Scales demo, values of either sign from a thousandth to a thousand on an arcsinh y axis with a slider for the linear threshold, from uPlot's `arcsinh-scales.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.ArcsinhScales.chart`, `yPixel`, `yValue`, at `arcsinh-scales/`.
- Show the Custom Scales demo, a Weibull plot with confidence bands on linear, log and log(-log(1 - y)) y scales, from uPlot's `custom-scales.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.CustomScales.Spec.chart`, `yPixel`, `yValue`, at `custom-scales/`.
- Show the Log Scales demo, twelve servers' player counts over a day on log and linear y axes with unreported samples as gaps, from uPlot's `log-scales.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.LogScales.specs`, `Spec.chart`, at `log-scales/`.
- Show the Log Scales 2 demo, the same data on linear, log10 and log2 y axes with zero and negative values as gaps, from uPlot's `log-scales2.html` (charts 1 to 4 and 7 to 10): `Inkproof.Demos.LogScales2.specs`, `Spec.chart`, at `log-scales2/`.
- Show the Axis Control demo, half a million points on a fixed y range with a red right axis and axis titles, from uPlot's `axis-control.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.AxisControl.chart`, at `axis-control/`.
- Show the Missing Data, High-Low Bands and Path Gap Clip demos, lines and bands over Unix-time x axes with gaps drawn as gaps, and stepped and spline joins, from uPlot's `missing-data.html`, `high-low-bands.html` and `path-gap-clip.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.Lines.Chart`, at `missing-data/`, `high-low-bands/` and `path-gap-clip/`.
- Show a million samples with twelve one-sample spikes drawn two ways, one sample per pixel column against the lowest and highest per column from Core's pyramid, and count the spikes each keeps: `Inkproof.Demos.Million.naiveChart`, `Million.inkproofChart`, at `million/`.
- Show three dense series of 55,550 samples as per-column envelopes, with the highest value of each in the data and as drawn: `Inkproof.Demos.ServerEvents.chart`, at `server-events/`.
- Zoom and pan a line chart with the wheel, a drag, a shift-drag box and a two-finger pinch, redrawing under each visible range from buffers uploaded once, and reset it to the full range, from uPlot's `zoom-wheel.html`, `zoom-touch.html` and `zoom-variations.html` and a reset button that uPlot's demos lack: `Inkproof.Demos.Zoom.Chart.commands`, `Zoom.Chart.config`, at `zoom-wheel/`, `zoom-touch/`, `zoom-variations/` and `zoom-reset-range/`.
- Link charts so a pointer move draws the cursor on all of them and a zoom moves them together, from uPlot's `sync-cursor.html`, with an unlink and relink button: `Inkproof.Demos.Linked.syncCharts`, at `sync-cursor/`.
- Draw a cursor at the data position under the pointer and show that position as a reading, from `cursor-bind.html`: `Linked.cursorBindChart`, at `cursor-bind/`; `?seed=<n>` fixes the random data.
- Zoom a chart to a dragged range and fetch the samples of that range from a data file by byte range, from `zoom-fetch.html`: `Linked.windowOf`, `Linked.points`, `Linked.fetched`, at `zoom-fetch/`.
- Select a range by dragging a small overview chart, and show it on a large chart that follows, from `zoom-ranger.html`: `Linked.rangerCharts`, at `zoom-ranger/`.
- Show open, high, low and close as candles with wicks and a volume bar on a second scale, from `candlestick-ohlc.html`: `Candles.candles`, `Candles.shapes`, at `candlestick/`.
- Show box and whisker plots of grouped runs, with quartiles, a median, whiskers and outliers, from `box-whisker.html`: `Boxes.Box.ofRuns`, `Boxes.charts`, at `box-whisker/`.
- Show discrete states as labelled bars in rows along a time axis, from `timeline-discrete.html`: `Timeline.Chart`, `Timeline.charts`, at `timeline-discrete/`, with a legend reading each row's state at the cursor (`Browser.rowGroup`, `Browser.rowStates`).
- Mark a data position or range on a zooming chart with a dashed line or shaded band and a label that stay where the data is, from `annotations.html`: `Zoom.Mark`, `Annotations.chart`, at `annotations/`.
- Show the Stacked Series demo, five stacked areas and four stacked bars with a gap and negative values, from uPlot's `stacked-series.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.Stacked.areaChart`, `Stacked.barChart`, at `stacked/`; `?seed=<n>` fixes the random bars.
- Show the Line Paths demo, one series drawn linear, as a monotone spline and stepped before or after each point, with a dot at each point (`Layer.dots`), from uPlot's `line-paths.html`: `Inkproof.Demos.LinePaths.charts`, at `line-paths/`.
- Show gradient strokes and fills on line charts, a colour ramp along x or y, blended or changing at once at each stop: `Lines.Gradient`, `Layer.strokeGradient`, `Layer.fillGradient`, and the uPlot gradients charts in `Inkproof.Demos.Gradients`, at `gradients/`.
- Give a page's charts a legend that reads each series at the cursor and hides it on a click, as uPlot's default legend does, drawing again from retained layers so that a click builds nothing again: `Browser.registerRetained`, `Browser.registerLines`, `Browser.Drawing`, `Pixels.drawing`, `Browser.registerLegend` (builds again per click), `Browser.seriesTraces`, `Browser.accessOf`, `Frame.viewConfig`, on the Annotations, Cursor Bind, Gradients, High Low Bands, Line Paths, Log Scales, Log Scales 2, Missing Data, Path Gap Clip, Points, Stacked, Sync Cursor and Zoom pages.
- Give the Candlestick and Box Whisker charts a legend that reads a group of values at the cursor, as uPlot's does: `Browser.paneGroup`, `Browser.paneFrame`, `Browser.paneScene`.
- Show the series and values of the data point nearest the pointer and a legend with each series at the cursor that hides or shows it, in the spirit of `tooltips-closest.html` and `legend-series.html`: `ZoomPage.mount` with the `tooltip` and `legend` attributes, at `tooltip-legend/`.

## Build and run

Needs git, Node 24 and [elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan). From the repository root, `scripts/build-site.sh` builds the whole site into `_site/`; serve that directory under `/inkproof-demos/` to see it as GitHub Pages does. `lake test` runs the native tests and the browser tests in headless Chromium.

## Credits

The demos recreate pages of [uPlot](https://github.com/leeoniya/uPlot) by Leon Sorokin (MIT license). Server events recreates the shape of uPlot's `bench/uPlot.html` data (three series over 55,550 samples) with generated values; uPlot has no demo of reduction accuracy. The Sparklines stock records are uPlot's `demos/data/*.csv`, the Multi-Bars library figures its `bench/results.json`, and the skew normal draw, from lean-random-sampling, follows its `demos/lib/rand.js`. The zoom pages draw uPlot's wheel and touch zoom data (seven points, two series) and a fixed hash for the variations page, whose points uPlot draws at random; uPlot 1.6.32 has no zoom-reset-range page, so that one is the wheel zoom chart with a reset button. A plain drag pans, where uPlot's variations page zooms to the dragged box; the box is a shift-drag. The sync-cursor page also links the x range, which uPlot's does not; cursor-bind has no counterpart of uPlot's Ctrl-drag annotation; zoom-fetch fetches from a generated data file (`lake exe demo-data`) where uPlot swaps in four fixed points; zoom-ranger draws generated data, and both charts show the same series. The candlestick page draws uPlot's gold prices (`demos/candlestick-ohlc.html`) with a fixed hash for the volumes it draws at random, widens the one day whose close exceeds its high, and has no tooltip or column highlight. The box-whisker page draws generated timings for 24 frameworks in three benchmarks where uPlot reads `demos/data/results.json`, with the same quartiles, whiskers and outliers; its labels turn only as far as Inkproof's axes turn them. The timeline page recreates the first three charts of uPlot's page, with times and states from a fixed hash where it draws them at random, and no hover highlight. The annotations page draws its notes at the data positions of the originals over a fixed hash of values, and its zoom is Inkproof's wheel and drag zoom where uPlot's has none.

The timezones-dst page draws uPlot's hourly 2019 data in the same twelve charts, with each zone's offsets from Axis's `posixZone` over a POSIX rule string, where uPlot reads the browser's zone database; its ticks sit at the zone's local clock intervals, and its cursors are not synchronised. The months page labels ticks with month names only, and plots the original's random levels from a seed. The arcsinh-scales page recreates the original's y axis and threshold slider, with ticks at the powers of ten either side of zero. uPlot 1.6.32 has no log-time-axis page, so there is none here. The custom-scales page generates the three curves from a Weibull fit and its delta-method bounds, fitted to uPlot's data, and takes the observed points from it; its Weibull axis drops the labels that would overlap.
